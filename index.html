How it works

Agora is an event listing platform that focuses on events that are In-Person, Cultural, Academic, Art-Focused, and mentally stimulating and engaging. We work hard to create lists of institutions, organizations, and event hosts in a city, and then we go through and curate the events we feel match our desired curation. We look at places like Universities, Think Tanks, Reperatory Cinemas, Small Playhouses, Museums, Public Libraries, Book Stores, Lecture Series Hosts, and whatever else we find!

Lectures & Panels

We review the event calendars of dozens of universities, think tanks, museums, local lecture series hosts, and more to find intellectually stimulating lectures, panels, and discussions from accredited speakers and organizations.

Films

We monitor the programming of the small, mom-and-pop, independent movie theaters in your city to put all the niche, arthouse, retro, or just straight up classics that are playing into one neat place.

Literature

Did you know your local book stores host readings and author talks all the time?! Well you do now. We compiled a list for you from all the local bookshops in your area, so you don't have to.

Music

There are a billion apps that tell you what concerts are in your area. We don't do that. What we DO, is tell you what operas, orchestras, symphonies, and other more niche musical performances are happening at a cultural institution near you.

Art

There are well over 100 museums in NYC alone, and nobody has time to check each and every one's exhibendar. We review museums across your city from major to boutique to make sure we have an up-to-date list of what exhibits are up and which are on the way! All in one place.

Other

We try to keep a generally open mind to a wide variety of events that could belong on the Agora listings. So in the Other category you'll find these. Have an idea? Send us a message below.
