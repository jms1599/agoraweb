Delete Account Instructions

1 Open the Agora app on your mobile phone.
2 Go to Profile.
3 Find and tap Manage Account.
4 Select the option to Delete Account and confirm.
This cannot be undone

Once you confirm, your account will be deleted and there is no way to recover it. Your data will be deleted.
